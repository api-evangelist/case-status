## Company Background
Case Status is the only intelligent client experience platform built for modern law firms. Founded in 2018 by Andy Seavers and Lauren Gulley, Case Status transforms how firms communicate, operate, and grow through AI-powered client intelligence, automation, and data-driven insights. Designed to integrate seamlessly with leading case management systems, Case Status delivers a branded, mobile-first experience that keeps clients informed, reduces manual work, and drives measurable results. By turning everyday communication into efficiency, satisfaction, and growth, Case Status empowers tech-forward law firms to scale operations, elevate client experience, and stay ahead of the competition.

## Core Service Offerings


### Primary Services
- Client Intelligence™ – AI-powered insights that surface message urgency, recommend responses, summarize case updates, translate client communication, and triage conversations in real time to protect satisfaction and efficiency.
- Client Satisfaction Analytics – Integrated tools to measure and predict satisfaction, including NPS, Predictive NPS, and CSAT, helping firms proactively manage client relationships and prevent churn.
- Branded Client App – A secure, fully branded mobile experience with your firm’s name and icon. Clients can access real-time updates, documents, and messages—anytime, anywhere—under your firm’s brand.
- Automations – Intelligent workflows that deliver automated status updates, messages, videos, and reminders—saving time while ensuring every client stays informed without manual effort.
- Messaging Hub – Secure, CMS-integrated communication with 1:1, bulk, and automated options—AI-supported, translated, and fully trackable. Reduce reliance on text, email, and phone by consolidating all client messaging in one place.
- Marketing That Drives Growth – Built-in automation for drip campaigns, Google reviews, and referrals that turn client satisfaction into measurable revenue growth.
- Statusphere – A collaborative network that allows firms to share client status securely with co-counsel, partners, and referring firms—extending communication beyond your walls while maintaining client transparency.
